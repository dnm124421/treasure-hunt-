— HOW IT WORKS

FOUR STEPS TO THE TREASURE

01

RECEIVE THE RIDDLE

Read your current encrypted clue on your smartphone. Every clue describes a physical location on campus.

02

SEARCH THE CAMPUS

Navigate to the physical landmark with your 4 teammates. Locate the secret alphanumeric sticker or card hidden at the spot.

03

ENTER THE CODE

Type the secret code into your team's live game console and press Submit. Hyphens and spaces are handled automatically.

04

UNLOCK NEXT CLUE

Instant verification unlocks your next waypoint. Complete all 6 clues within 30 minutes to record your final mission time.
